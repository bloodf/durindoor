## Security note on #3313

PARTIAL, and the remainder is the part that matters. The fork already routes every provider-node probe
through `guardedProbeFetch` with `redirect: "manual"`, so literal private/metadata targets and redirect
bypasses are closed. But `open-sse/utils/outboundUrlGuard.js` classifies hostname **text** only and says
so in its own comment, and DNS resolution happens later at transport — so a hostname that resolves to a
private or metadata address still gets through. Closing it needs resolved-address pinning in an Undici
dispatcher, not another hostname check. Shipping the hostname half alone as a "SSRF fix" would be worse
than leaving it open, because it stops anyone looking again.
