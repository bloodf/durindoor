---
title: Bulk connection selection
description: Select and review connections across pages and filters.
---


On a provider's dashboard, filter connections by name or email and use the page controls to browse matches. **Select this page** affects only the displayed page. Selections remain active when you change pages or filters; the selected count includes hidden selections. Expand **Review selected connections** before applying a bulk action to see its complete scope. Clear the selection when you want to start over.
