# Operational metrics coverage (#1129)

`tests/unit/1129-operational-metrics.test.js` exercises the authenticated scrape handler with in-process synthetic observations, a mocked authorization decision and active-request snapshot, a controlled event-loop histogram, and the real in-process concurrency gate. It checks HTTP outcome and latency totals, a published provider-health aggregate, queue depth, a simulated stall and bounded diagnostic history. It makes no live provider calls. The existing `operational-metrics-auth.test.js` covers credential verification through middleware and the handler; `operational-metrics-runtime.test.js` covers additional recorder lifecycle and invalid-reading cases.

This focused suite does not establish real provider availability, end-to-end route coverage, actual management credentials, process restart behavior, real event-loop sampling overhead, runtime boot integration, cross-process aggregation, or production deployment behavior. Its synthetic stall is not a measured event-loop delay. The health projection is injected, not a live health probe. It does not establish complete secret exclusion across all routes or artifacts. The issue's label-contract, authorization and scraping documentation, restart/privacy, overhead, pressure correlation and unusable-reading acceptance remain subject to the full issue evidence and independent review; this suite alone does not close any checklist item.
