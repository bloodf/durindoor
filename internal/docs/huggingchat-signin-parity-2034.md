## Pinned runtime and remaining dispatch gap

The later [OmniRoute executor at 4624250231f49ed118fb4522a9d735e4894322c6](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/blob/4624250231f49ed118fb4522a9d735e4894322c6/open-sse/executors/huggingchat.ts) copies an `hf-chat` cookie and posts to `/chat/conversation`, reads `/chat/api/v2/conversations/{id}`, then posts multipart data to `/chat/conversation/{id}` and translates JSONL. It merges response cookies. The [Chat UI documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/chat-ui/index) describes deployment and OpenID, not an external cookie-based integration contract. Its [conversation API](https://github.com/huggingface/chat-ui/blob/main/src/routes/api/v2/conversations/%5Bid%5D/%2Bserver.ts) calls `requireAuth` and resolves the conversation against the session; [requireAuth](https://github.com/huggingface/chat-ui/blob/main/src/lib/server/api/utils/requireAuth.ts) accepts a local user or session. Public source establishes internal route behavior, not authorization for an external client to reuse the web session. It also does not establish the hosted deployment's complete access controls or cookie-rotation contract. Therefore normal dispatch remains closed; no account or route-level parity is claimed. Hugging Face inference tokens remain a separate provider.

The isolated parser now bounds total input to 4 MiB, as in OmniRoute's [pinned JSONL translator](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/blob/4624250231f49ed118fb4522a9d735e4894322c6/open-sse/executors/huggingchat/jsonlStream.ts), reports over-limit responses as failures, and cancels stalled reads on abort. Existing malformed-line and generation-error behavior remains covered. This parser hardening does not enable the executor.
