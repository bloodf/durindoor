# Muse sign-in comparison (#2034)

Snapshots: [CLIProxyAPI 6faa8d2](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/tree/6faa8d20b2673404cff70a2f2d9537ae0d0e684d), [EasyCLIProxyAPI e4223d5](https://github.com/router-for-me/EasyCLIProxyAPI/tree/e4223d5eefa22a3301ca7e2a28d05897432b07f9), and [OmniRoute e3c1531](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/tree/e3c1531afd07d5e06f7ffab658584a7ae2e3627e).

CLIProxyAPI's [Meta authentication](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/blob/6faa8d20b2673404cff70a2f2d9537ae0d0e684d/internal/auth/meta/meta.go) uses a device grant followed by a best-effort key mint; its [authenticator](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/blob/6faa8d20b2673404cff70a2f2d9537ae0d0e684d/sdk/auth/meta.go) retains the device token and disables scheduled refresh of minted keys. Its [stream executor](https://github.com/router-for-me/CLIProxyAPI/blob/6faa8d20b2673404cff70a2f2d9537ae0d0e684d/internal/runtime/executor/meta_executor_stream.go) handles Responses events and filters subscription usage events. EasyCLIProxyAPI's [management API](https://github.com/router-for-me/EasyCLIProxyAPI/blob/e4223d5eefa22a3301ca7e2a28d05897432b07f9/src-tauri/src/management_api.rs) delegates OAuth to its core; it does not establish an independent inference contract.

OmniRoute's [OAuth adapter](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/blob/e3c1531afd07d5e06f7ffab658584a7ae2e3627e/src/lib/oauth/providers/muse-code.ts) also mints and retains a device token. Its [model discovery](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/blob/e3c1531afd07d5e06f7ffab658584a7ae2e3627e/open-sse/services/museCodeModels.ts) merges the key-specific `/v1/models` listing with `/muse-code/models` metadata. Its [usage service](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/blob/e3c1531afd07d5e06f7ffab658584a7ae2e3627e/open-sse/services/usage/museCode.ts) reports unknown usage and retained subscription metadata.

The closed-mock test exercises two local sign-ins through the provider entry points and checks that each mint and bearer key stays with its own credential. It does not establish model discovery or streaming parity, or validate live provider behavior. Further investigation and regression coverage are required for those paths.
