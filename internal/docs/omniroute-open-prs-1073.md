Provider-add PRs whose ids already exist locally (TinyFish fetch, MiniMax, Muse Code, Adapta, fal-ai, SenseNova, Qoder, Devin, Cloudflare AI vs Playground) stay as **candidates** when the title is a behavior change, not a first-time add.

## Method

- **import** — bugfix or small gap on a seam DurinDoor already has. Read the diff, then port.
- **maybe** — new provider, dashboard, opt-in policy, or mixed train. Live-verify / sift first.
- **skip** — wrong product surface (UI kit rewrite, Grok Bot, Nix, gamification, 420k-line orphan) or an OmniRoute-only ops feature.

Risk is from diff size and surface (OAuth, web-session, CSP, identity), not from a review.
