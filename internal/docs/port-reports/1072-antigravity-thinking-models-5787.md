# 1072: Antigravity model IDs and thinking

Source: [9router PR 5787](https://github.com/decolua/9router/pull/5787), head `6ceccc1bf0a7074ac542e4119500a696b247d372` (open at review). Its catalog changes propose Carbon high, Argon high, Neon and Hydrogen low through xhigh with high defaults, and Claude 5.5 low through max with medium defaults. Its resolver changes preserve xhigh/max and its thinking changes put Claude thinking in the Antigravity envelope.

[Google's AI developer tools documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/ai-developer-tools-settings) lists the four Gemini families for Antigravity. [Google's thinking documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/thinking) specifies low, medium, high and xhigh for Hydrogen and Neon, and low, medium and high for Carbon and Argon. These documents do not establish the proposed tier-suffixed Antigravity wire IDs or Claude 5.5 mappings. The upstream PR is evidence of its proposed implementation, not independent verification of those routes.

DurinDoor already registers Hydrogen tiers, Carbon tiers, Argon tiers and Claude 5.5 low/medium/high, with clean wire IDs and separate thinking presets. The bare Hydrogen alias intentionally resolves to medium; Carbon resolves to medium, Argon to high, and both Claude aliases to high. Keep these established user contracts. Neon and Claude max rows, default changes and Claude envelope changes are deferred pending relevant primary-source evidence. No pricing, quota, authentication, integrations or MITM changes are adopted.

Hydrogen's existing xhigh row was unreachable through a bare alias plus xhigh intent and its thinking was reduced to high. The Antigravity-only change retains xhigh in catalog selection and the Gemini envelope, with high fallback for existing catalogs without an xhigh row. Its capability metadata advertises the documented Hydrogen levels, while explicitly supplied capabilities remain authoritative as in #2094. #2097's gateway image/stream behavior is unrelated and remains untouched. Gemini Interactions and other providers retain their existing behavior. Acceptance exercises source and destination IDs, presets and the executor envelope offline; it does not establish live provider acceptance.

The earlier OmniRoute Interactions pin (`tests/unit/omni-hydrogen-interactions-effort.test.js`) asserted the pre-#5787 Antigravity level set (low|medium|high) and was left stale by this port, so the suite failed on the branch base. The #1073 unit reconciled that pin to the Antigravity contract this port establishes (low|medium|high|xhigh) and records it in `1073-hydrogen-interactions-effort.md`; the merged tree keeps that expectation and adds the xhigh tier row, so the Antigravity level set is asserted in the Interactions fixture as well as in the #5787 coverage. The Gemini Interactions route keeps its own levels, and its alias-resolution and maxOutput pins are unchanged.
