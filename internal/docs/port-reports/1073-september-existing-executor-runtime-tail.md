## Defensive transport review

The #14959 design is limited to 429 retry decisions and does not change credentials, account scopes, or sign-in. Its source helper's unbounded cloned-body read is the material transport-boundary concern: a retry decision should not buffer an arbitrarily large provider error body. The local bounded optional-probe pattern is the appropriate acceptance constraint. The #14915 retry must remain one-shot, transport-specific, and serialize the exact outbound body through the local wire serializer if ever adapted. No source-only provider integration is evidence to add account credentials or broaden authentication behavior.
