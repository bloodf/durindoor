## Limits and open items

- **#19721 and later successes.** A success clears the lock, so an older failure cannot bring it back. If a still newer failure then locks the same scope again, a failure dispatched before that success can extend the new lock.
- **#19720 forced stream-to-JSON.** The streaming translator, the non-streaming reader and the ledger have tests. The forced stream-to-JSON handoff has no test of its own.
- **#19703 translated streams.** Only the passthrough path changed. An OpenAI upstream cut during translation to another client format was not checked.
